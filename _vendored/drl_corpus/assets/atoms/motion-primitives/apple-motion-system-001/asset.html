Motion primitives that feel immediate but quiet.

Looping specimens show travel, duration, and easing as live behavior while tokens carry the implementation values.

travel

State changes use short travel and low drama.

duration

Fast interface updates and slower panel reveals share one scale.

ease

Easing favors soft arrival over theatrical movement.

Preserve the principle: motion should confirm a change, guide attention, and then get out of the way.
